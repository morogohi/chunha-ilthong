### Description

```
An ink-wash map. Six warlords. One realm to unite.

Chunha is a turn-based Three Kingdoms strategy game where every turn is one month.
Pick one of six warlords and unify twelve cities through governance, recruitment, diplomacy, schemes and war.

■ Classic strategy, made for one hand
· Farm, trade, flood control and walls; draft and train your troops
· Visit hidden talents three times to win them over; recruit, release or execute captives
· Alliances, sabotage, fire attacks and one-on-one duels

■ A living ink painting
· Rivers flow and faction ink bleeds across the map as cities fall
· Petals, rain, leaves and snow follow the seasons
· Charges shake the screen; fire attacks fill it with flame and smoke

■ Short sessions, deep decisions
· Portrait, one-handed, 30–60 minute campaigns
· Three-turn undo, fully offline
· The court historian writes your chronicle — share it when you finish

No ads · No data collected · Plays offline
```
